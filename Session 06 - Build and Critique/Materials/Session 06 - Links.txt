Export controls on advanced chips will likely slow China's frontier AI development for several years. Chinese labs currently depend on Nvidia hardware for large training runs, and domestic alternatives appear to lag by at least a generation. Although smuggling and cloud access may blunt the effect somewhat, it seems unlikely that these channels can substitute at scale. The controls therefore probably buy the United States meaningful lead time, which policymakers should use to build governance capacity.
